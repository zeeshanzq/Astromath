🔬 Astro-Bot's Method Lab

Learn WHEN to use each Swedish & British math tool so you never get stuck!

1. Make-10 (Tiokompisar)

Best for: 7, 8, or 9 + single digit

When to use: When a number is close to the next ten (like 8 + 5 or 19 + 6).

Example: 8 + 5 → 8's ten-friend (tiokompis) is 2. Split 5 into 2 + 3. First 8 + 2 = 10, then 10 + 3 = 13!

2. Number Line (Tallinje)

Best for: Missing ? & Subtraction

When to use: Finding the jump gap in missing numbers (14 + ? = 22) or hopping backward when subtracting.

Example: 14 + ? = 22 → Start at 14. Jump +6 to reach friendly 20, then jump +2 to 22. Total jump = 6 + 2 = 8!

3. Split Tens & Ones (Partitioning)

Best for: Mental 2-digit sums

When to use: Adding two 2-digit numbers mentally when the ones don't overflow 10.

Example: 34 + 25 → Add Tens first: 30 + 20 = 50. Add Ones next: 4 + 5 = 9. Put together: 59!

4. Column Method (Uppställning)

Best for: Carrying & Bigger numbers

When to use: Bigger numbers where ones make 10 or more (like 47 + 36) so carrying stays neat.

Rule: Line up Tens (T) and Ones (O) vertically. Always add the Ones column on the right first!
